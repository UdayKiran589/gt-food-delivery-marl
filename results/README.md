# GT Food Delivery MARL — Research Results

This directory contains selected CSV summaries from the project's Model 2,
Model 4, and Model 5 experiments.

These files are preserved experimental outputs. Their presence does not
independently establish that every experiment has been reproduced or that
the reported differences are statistically significant.

## Model 2 — MAPPO, Forecasting, and Shock Studies

- `model2/evaluation_results.csv`: Evaluation metrics for the configurations
  recorded in the source results, including returns, profit, fulfillment,
  delay, cancellation, and risk-related measures.
- `model2/forecast_comparison.csv`: Seed-level comparison of returns with
  and without forecasting.
- `model2/forecast_quality.csv`: Forecasting error metrics for the listed
  forecasting methods and baselines.
- `model2/shock_study.csv`: Recorded normal-versus-shock metrics for the
  listed configurations.

The forecast-quality results include seasonal-naive performance that is
better than the listed LSTM and Transformer on MAE and RMSE. Results should
be interpreted using the metrics and evaluation setup recorded by the
original experiment.

## Model 4 — Adaptive Control

- `model4/model4_normal_shock_evaluation.csv`: Recorded metrics for the
  frozen Model 3 baseline, adaptive control, and full Model 4 configurations
  under normal and shock conditions.

**Limitation:** The provenance of the Model 3 checkpoint used for comparison
has not yet been fully resolved. Treat these comparisons as provisional
until checkpoint lineage and the evaluation setup are verified.

## Model 5 — XAI and Shock Influence

- `model5/model5_normal_vs_genuine_shock_influence.csv`: Aggregate metrics
  comparing normal and genuine-shock conditions across 12 recorded episodes
  per condition.

The saved summary reports a higher mean risk under genuine shock. This is a
descriptive result; it does not by itself establish improved policy
performance, causal attribution, or statistical significance.

## Reproducibility and interpretation

- These CSVs are copied from existing experiment outputs; they have not
  been regenerated by this repository update.
- Episode counts, seeds, configurations, and evaluation details should be
  checked against the corresponding experiment code and logs before making
  strong claims.
- Do not interpret a higher reward, lower risk, or other metric in isolation
  without checking its definition and the experimental conditions.
- Checkpoint provenance and external dataset or environment dependencies may
  limit full reproduction from a clean clone.
- Large raw logs, checkpoints, and detailed per-step outputs are not included
  here by default.

See `../documentation/REPRODUCIBILITY.md`, `../documentation/MODEL_STATUS.md`,
and `../documentation/EXPERIMENT_LOG.md` for the broader project context.
